DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Updated October 15, 2026 · Applies to Fever Diary 1.0

Fever Diary is provided by Hanqiu. This policy explains where your entries are stored, when they are shared, and how you can manage them.

Information you enter

You may enter profile nicknames, optional avatars, temperature readings and measurement methods, measurement times, medicine names and strengths, doses, medication times, and notes. For an avatar, the system photo picker gives the app only the photo you select. The app processes that photo without reading your entire photo library. The app also keeps entry identifiers, author identifiers, modification times, and profile settings for editing, grouping, and synchronization. Only record information you are authorized to manage. A parent or guardian should manage a child’s profile.

On-device storage and iCloud

The app stores entries on your device so you can view and work with them. Selected avatar photos are resized and saved locally within the corresponding profile. When you enable iCloud features, the relevant entries and settings, including avatars, are stored and synchronized through Apple CloudKit. We do not operate a separate server that receives your health entries or send them to advertising or analytics services.

iCloud availability, accounts, storage, and service processing are subject to Apple’s settings and terms. See Apple’s privacy policy. Your iOS and iCloud backup settings may separately control device backups.

Family collaboration

Only a profile you actively choose is shared with invited participants. With iCloud enabled, a shared profile’s avatar is shared along with that profile. Participants can read its entries, medicine presets, and settings, including its avatar and entry-author information. The app’s normal interface only permits editing or deleting entries a person added themselves. The profile creator manages participants and profile settings.

Sharing permission boundary: iCloud editable sharing grants write access to the shared data as a whole. It cannot enforce author-only editing for individual entries on the server. The app’s author checks are not a server-side security boundary. Only grant editable access to people you trust.

Stopping sharing or removing a participant revokes future cloud access. It cannot recall screenshots, exports, or other copies that person already saved.

Content you choose to share or export

Chart images, PDF files, and CSV files are generated on your device. Chart images may include a profile nickname, temperatures, dates, medicine names, and doses. They do not show entry-author names by default. PDF and CSV exports can cover an episode, a date range, or all history for the selected profile. They contain entries currently saved on the device, including family contributions, along with the profile nickname, original temperature readings, medicine details, and notes. Avatars and entry-author names are omitted.

The app temporarily stores generated files for previewing, saving, or sharing. Content is handed to a destination only when you choose a location, app, or service in the system save or share interface. Subsequent handling depends on that service’s policies and your settings. Images, PDFs, and CSVs already saved or sent are not automatically updated by later changes or deletions in the app, and the app cannot recall them.

Purchases and restoring purchases

Premium is a one-time, non-consumable App Store purchase with no automatic renewal. Apple processes payments. The app uses StoreKit to verify entitlements and refund or revocation status. We do not receive card numbers or payment passwords. Apple may provide developers with aggregate sales reports or diagnostics that users have agreed to share.

No advertising or third-party tracking

The app has no advertising SDK, third-party behavioral analytics SDK, or cross-app advertising tracking. It does not access Contacts, location, or HealthKit data. We do not sell your health entries.

Deletion, retention, and control

You can delete entries you created and use the available profile controls to delete your own profiles, leave a share, or stop sharing. Removing synchronized data requires a connection and completion of iCloud synchronization.

To erase all data, go to Settings → Data management → Delete all data, read the explanation, and type the confirmation text. This clears local profiles, avatars, temperature and medication entries, medicine presets, recorder name, pending sync caches, and export files temporarily stored by the app. Even when sync is off, it checks and deletes your owned profiles and legacy backup in this app’s current iCloud account. Profiles you created and shared are deleted in full, including family contributions. For profiles shared with you, your cloud entries are deleted and you leave the share; the creator’s and other participants’ data remain.

Full deletion requires a connection and the current iCloud account. Completion is shown only after cloud and local deletion both succeed. If interrupted, the app pauses editing and sync; keep the same account and retry. Deletion does not affect Premium purchases, other apps’ iCloud data, system backups, or images, PDFs, CSVs, and other copies already saved by you or others. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete iCloud data or system backups.

The app restricts access when it detects that sharing permission has ended. Offline devices may retain previously downloaded copies. Use the relevant iCloud controls to manage system backups. Apple retains purchase records under its own policies.

Support emails and this website

If you email support, your message contains the email address, description, and attachments you choose to provide. They are used to address your request. Please avoid sending unnecessary health information. We keep support messages for as long as needed to address the issue, and you may request their deletion using the address below.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no added analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub may process IP addresses and access logs for hosting and security. See the GitHub privacy statement.
