Frequently asked questions

How do I quickly record a temperature?

Select a health profile, open the temperature entry form, enter the reading, and save. The time defaults to now and can be changed for an earlier reading. Enter one or two ear readings without labeling left and right, or one underarm reading. The profile’s default measurement method is preselected and can be changed for this entry.

Why can I record medicine by selecting it?

The profile creator can save medicines with a usual dose and unit in settings. A quick entry copies that preset dose, which you can still adjust before saving. Changing a preset does not change past medication entries. You supply these presets; they are not dosage advice from the app.

How are illness episodes grouped?

Temperature and medicine entries are sorted together by the time they occurred. A gap equal to or greater than the profile’s interval begins a new group. The default is 48 hours. Changing the interval or adding, editing, or deleting entries recalculates past groups. Grouping organizes a diary; it does not determine medical recovery.

What is free, and what does Premium include?

The free plan includes one personal profile, temperature and medicine entries, full history, chart-image sharing, and PDF/CSV exports. A one-time Premium purchase unlocks multiple profiles and family collaboration, without automatic renewal. Each collaboration participant needs Premium. Apple’s purchase confirmation sheet shows the final price.

How do I restore Premium on another phone?

Use the Apple Account that originally purchased Premium, open the Premium screen, and select “Restore purchases.” Purchase restoration uses the App Store and is separate from synchronizing health entries. Check your account and connection if restoration fails. For refunds, use Apple’s report-a-problem page.

Why have family entries not updated?

Check that both people are signed in to iCloud, have accepted the profile invitation, have an internet connection and available iCloud storage, and have Premium. Reopen the app or use its synchronization action to check for updates. Sync can be delayed and should not be relied on for real-time monitoring. Read the privacy policy for editable-sharing permission limits.

Does Fever Diary measure temperature or recommend doses?

No. The app organizes entries you enter manually. It does not connect to a thermometer, perform real-time monitoring, or provide diagnoses, medication schedules, or dosage recommendations. Ask an appropriate healthcare professional about treatment and medicine.

How do I export a PDF or CSV?

Select a profile and open export from History, or go to Settings → Data management → Export records. Choose an episode, date range, or all history for the current member, then select PDF or CSV and generate the file. PDFs contain trends and record details for reading or printing. CSVs contain one row per entry for use in a spreadsheet. You can then preview, save to Files, or share the result.

Exports use entries currently saved on this device, including family contributions. Complete any needed sync first. Files include the nickname, temperatures, medicine details, and notes, without avatars or entry-author names. Review the contents before sending; the app cannot recall saved or shared files.

How do I delete data or stop sharing?

Delete entries you created using their entry controls. Use profile management to delete your own profiles, leave a share, or manage invited participants. Connect to the internet to synchronize deletions.

To erase everything, go to Settings → Data management → Delete all data, read the affected scope, and type the confirmation text. This clears local data and checks and deletes this app’s data in the current iCloud account, even when sync is off. Shared profiles you created are deleted in full. For profiles shared with you, your cloud entries are deleted and you leave; other people’s data remain. A connection and the current iCloud account are required. If deletion is incomplete, keep the same account and retry until completion is shown.

Your Premium purchase is retained. Exported files, copies saved by others, other apps’ data, and system backups are unaffected. Uninstalling the app does not automatically remove iCloud data. See the privacy policy for details.
